# RISEUP Daily Sales Report

Person B fills it in at 17:30, using the CRM Dashboard "Today" column and the Daily Plan row. Both people review it for 10 minutes at 18:00. Copy the block below into the team chat or a doc each day.

## Daily review questions (18:00, 10 minutes)
1. How much cash arrived and was verified today? What exactly will pay tomorrow?
2. Who said "yes" but hasn't received an invoice yet? (Send it now.)
3. Which proposals are older than 48 h without an answer? (Follow up first thing tomorrow.)
4. Does every active lead have an owner, a next action and a date?
5. Which message or channel produced the replies today?
6. What one thing do we stop or change tomorrow?
