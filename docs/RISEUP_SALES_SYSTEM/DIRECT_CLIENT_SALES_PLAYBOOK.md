## 1. ICP
- High inbound volume: **30+ messages/calls a week**, with the business visibly answering late in Instagram comments or reviews.
- Owner or manager reachable by phone or Instagram.
- Prices are public, or there's a booking/ordering process today (calls, DMs, Google Forms).
- Has a physical location or delivery in Yerevan or a major city.

## 2. Qualification (BANT-lite)
| Ask | Good answer |
|---|---|
| "How many messages do you get a week? Who answers at night/weekends?" | 30+; nobody at night |
| "How many inquiries do you lose or answer late?" | "Some / many" |
| "Who decides on this?" | The person you're talking to |
| "If this pays for itself with 2–3 extra bookings a month, is it worth trying this month?" | Yes |

## 3. Outreach
| Step | How |
|---|---|
| 1. Prepare | B builds the list: Instagram + Google Maps, ≥ 4.0 rating, active account. Note one observation, e.g. "reply time 1 day on the post from 20 Nov" |
| 2. First contact | Instagram DM or WhatsApp in Armenian (Scripts §2) + a short L2 video |
| 3. Call | A calls the same or next day (Scripts §3). Phone works better than DM in Armenia |
| 4. Follow-ups | 2/4/7/14 days (Scripts §4) |
| 5. Walk-in | Optional for nearby businesses in Yerevan: a 2-minute visit, show the demo on a phone |

## 4. Discovery questions
1. How do customers book or order today? Which channels?
2. What happens to messages after hours?
3. What questions do you answer 10 times a day?
4. Which CRM/calendar/Excel do you use?
5. What would one more booking or order a day be worth to you?

## 5. Demo (10 min, on the phone or a laptop)
1. Send a test message as a "customer" to the L2 demo.
2. Show the AI reply, booking, and the Telegram notification to the owner.
3. Show the daily report.
4. "For you it would answer about [their services/prices]. Setup takes 3–5 days."

## 6. Proposal and close
- `docs/RISEUP_BUSINESS_LAUNCH/DIRECT_CLIENT_PROPOSAL.md` (hy/en). Send the invoice in the same message.
- **Payment:** ≤ 100,000 ֏ → 100% upfront. Larger projects → 50/50. Monthly fee prepaid.
- **Close:** "Shall we start on [date]? I'll send the invoice for the deposit."

## 7. Objections specific to direct clients
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "AI will answer wrongly" | "It answers only from your approved FAQ list; anything else goes to you in Telegram." |
| "Our admin does this" | "The admin keeps the calls. The assistant covers nights, weekends and the repeat questions." |
| "Too expensive" | Offer the Entry package (Start 45k) first, then upgrade |
