# RISEUP Sales Strategy: $5,000 Collected in 30 Days

## Position
> **RISEUP helps digital agencies deliver more client projects without hiring additional developers. We work behind the scenes under your brand.**

For direct businesses:
> **RISEUP answers your customers 24/7, books appointments and takes orders automatically, so no inquiry is lost.**

## Ideal customer profiles (summary; details are in the playbooks)
- **Agency:** 3–30 staff; sells websites, marketing or CRM; no in-house developers, or they're overloaded; active this month (posts, job ads); English, Russian or Armenian.
- **Direct:** beauty/clinic, restaurant/shop or real estate business. Signs to look for: 30+ inquiries a week on Instagram/WhatsApp, an owner who can be reached, prices that are public or easy to ask about.
- **Upwork:** payment verified, ≥ $1k spent, hire rate ≥ 50%, clear scope, fixed budget ≥ $200 or hourly ≥ $30.
- **Creator:** ≥ 10k engaged followers; a real product idea; willing to pay the validation sprint.

## Funnel and responsibilities
`Lead → verify → first contact → follow-ups (2/4/7/14 d) → reply → qualify → call/demo → proposal (same day) → invoice → verified payment → delivery → referral/upsell`

| Who | Stages |
|---|---|
| Person B | Stages 1–7 (New Lead → Interested), scheduling, proposal tracking, payment reminders |
| Person A | Stages 8–14 (Demo → Paid), delivery |

## Rules
1. **Cash rule:** only money seen in the bank, Payoneer or Upwork counts (CRM Payments → Verified = Yes).
2. **Same-day rule:** proposal within 24 h of a call; invoice within 1 h of a "yes".
3. **Next-action rule:** every open lead has an owner, a next action and a date.
4. **No free work:** paid test minimum of $250 / 100,000 ֏.
5. **Price rule:** approved prices are unchanged. Discounts follow `OBJECTION_HANDLING.md` only.
6. **Focus rule:** after the Day 14 review, put 70% of time into the channel with the best contact → paid rate.
