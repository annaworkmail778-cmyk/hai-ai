# RISEUP Creator (Blogger) Partnership Playbook (secondary channel)

**Rule:** we build nothing before **paid validation**. The creator pays the sprint, or pre-sales prove demand.
- Maximum 10% of outreach time.
- Agreement: `docs/RISEUP_BUSINESS_LAUNCH/BLOGGER_REVENUE_SHARE_AGREEMENT.md`.

## ICP
- 10k–300k followers with real engagement (≥ 2% likes or comments).
- Niche: education, fitness, beauty, business, cooking.
- Already sells something, or audience comments ask for a course, guide or app.

## Offer
| Step | What | Price |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Validation sprint | 7 days: landing page + waitlist/pre-order, a story script, a demand report | **$300 / 120,000 ֏, 100% upfront** |
| 2. Build (only if validated) | ≥ 30 pre-orders or ≥ 300 waitlist sign-ups | Fixed price per scope, or reduced price + revenue share 10/25/40% (per the agreement) |

## Scoring add-ons
| Score | When |
|---|---|
| "Ability to pay" = 5 | The creator already has sales |
| "Urgency" = 5 | A launch date is set |

## Outreach
- DM: the creator scripts in `docs/HAI_AI_30_DAY/SALES_SCRIPTS.md` §4.
- Follow-ups: 2/4/7/14.

## Qualification
1. What do your followers ask you for most?
2. Have you sold anything before? How many buyers?
3. Can you invest $300 to test demand before building?

A "no" to Q3 → Follow-up Later. **No free builds.**

## Close
Send the sprint invoice. Start after verified payment.
