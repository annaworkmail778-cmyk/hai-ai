# RISEUP Business OS: In-person Sales Demo Script (Tsaghkadzor)

**Meeting format:** 15–20 minutes total.

| Part | Time |
|---|---|
| Discovery (listen first) | 5–7 min |
| Demo of the one vertical that fits | 3–5 min |
| Their numbers in the ROI calculator | 3 min |
| Pilot offer and next step | 3 min |

**Language:** Armenian by default; switch to Russian if the owner prefers.

**Equipment:** a tablet or laptop with the demo pre-loaded (works offline), a phone, printed QR card, pilot offer sheet.

**Rules:**
- Say "demo data" and "simulated" out loud when showing numbers and messages.
- Never promise revenue.
- Never imply the demo hotel/restaurant is a real client.
- Show the HosDog/Bistro case only with written permission.

## 1. Before the meeting (10 min)
- [ ] Research the business: website?, Instagram activity, Booking.com/Google rating, review complaints ("slow reply", "waited long").
- [ ] Note one specific observation to mention.
- [ ] Press **Reset demo**. Choose the guided script. Set the language.
- [ ] Battery > 70%; offline mode checked.

## 2. Opening (30 seconds)
**HY:** «Բարև Ձեզ, ես [Name]-ն եմ RISEUP-ից։ Մենք Ծաղկաձորի հյուրանոցներին ու ռեստորաններին օգնում ենք չկորցնել հաճախորդների հարցումներն ու պատվերները և ժամանակ խնայել ամենօրյա աշխատանքում։ Չեմ եկել ինչ-որ բան վաճառելու առաջին րոպեից. ուզում եմ հասկանալ, թե ինչպես եք հիմա աշխատում։ 5 րոպե ունե՞ք»
**RU:** «Здравствуйте, я [Name] из RISEUP. Мы помогаем отелям и ресторанам Цахкадзора не терять заявки и заказы и экономить время на рутине. Сначала хочу понять, как у вас всё устроено сейчас. Есть 5 минут?»

## 3. Discovery questions (pick 5–6)
**Hotels:**
1. How do guests reach you: phone, Instagram, WhatsApp, Booking.com, website? Which brings the most?
2. Who answers at night and on weekends? How fast?
3. What share of bookings comes through Booking/Airbnb? What does the commission cost you in high season?
4. How do you keep availability in sync across channels? Ever had a double booking?
5. How does reception tell housekeeping which rooms to clean?
6. How do you know tonight's occupancy and this month's revenue?
7. Do guests come back? Do you contact them after the stay?

**Restaurants / cafés:**
1. What happens at the busiest hour? Where do orders get delayed or mixed up?
2. How do orders reach the kitchen: paper, voice, POS printer?
3. How often does the menu change? Are the printed menus up to date?
4. Do you take pickup or delivery orders? How: by phone?
5. Do you know your best-selling and most profitable dishes?
6. Do you know your regular guests? Do you do anything for them?

**Qualifying (both):**
- Who decides on such tools?
- Have you tried a system before? What didn't work?
- If something solved [their pain], when would you want it working: before the New Year peak?

**Write down their words.** Use them in the demo ("you said the night messages…").

## 4. Hotel demo (3–5 minutes)
Use the guided mode, `DEMO_UX_FLOWS.md` §2.
1. **Site (20 s):** "Your own site, in three languages, built for phones. Guests come from Google and Instagram directly to you."
2. **23:30 message (60 s):** play the Russian-speaking family asking for 12–14 January. "It answers from **your** prices and rules. It doesn't confirm; it collects the request and tells the guest when you'll reply."
3. **Inbox (30 s):** "Everything lands here: assistant, site, phone calls you enter. Nothing stays in someone's personal phone."
4. **Approve in the calendar (30 s):** "You choose the room and approve. One click."
5. **Automation timeline (30 s):** "Confirmation, pre-arrival, check-in instructions and the feedback request go out automatically. **In the demo nothing is sent**; in your system they'd go via Telegram, email or WhatsApp."
6. **Housekeeping (20 s):** "Checkout creates the cleaning task; reception sees when the room is ready."
7. **Analytics (30 s):** "Demo numbers: occupancy, average rate, revenue per room, how much came direct versus Booking."
8. **Bridge to them:** "You said [their pain]. This is the part that addresses it."

## 5. Restaurant demo (3–5 minutes)
`DEMO_UX_FLOWS.md` §3.
1. **QR (30 s):** hand them the phone or the QR card. "The guest at table 7 opens the menu in their language."
2. **Order (45 s):** add dishes, a note "no onions", send. "Notice it says *waiting for the restaurant to accept*; we never pretend."
3. **Manager view (30 s):** the order pops in. Accept.
4. **Kitchen screen (60 s):** ticket with timer → Preparing → Ready; the guest's phone updates. "No paper, no shouting, timers show what's late."
5. **Sold out (20 s):** "Gata is finished → one tap → gone from the menu."
6. **Numbers (45 s):** today's sales, best sellers, peak hours, food cost per dish. "Demo data; with yours you'd see this daily."

## 6. Their numbers (3 minutes)
Open the ROI calculator. Ask for their inquiries per week (or orders per day), their estimate of lost requests (or errors), and the average booking (or order) value.
- "These are your estimates. Let's be conservative and take the low end."
- If the benefit doesn't exceed the cost: say so. Suggest Starter only, or not now.

## 7. Objection handling
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We use Booking.com, it's enough" | "Keep it. The goal is to also get guests who book directly, without commission, and not lose those who write at night. Booking stays." |
| "We have a POS / cash register" | "We don't replace it or the fiscal receipt. The QR menu and kitchen screen work beside it." |
| "My staff won't use it" | "That's why the pilot is 30 days with training; the screens have 3–4 buttons. If they don't use it, you stop." |
| "AI will tell guests wrong things" | "It only answers from the information you approve, and passes everything else to a person. It can't confirm a booking." |
| "Too expensive" | "Start with Starter or the 100,000 ֏ pilot. Decide with your own numbers after 30 days." |
| "We're too busy now, it's the season" | "That's when lost requests cost most. The pilot takes about an hour of your time; we do the setup." |
| "Is my data safe?" | "Your data is visible only to your staff accounts; we sign a data-processing agreement." |
| "Show me a real client" | Honest: "This is a demo business. We've built ordering and dashboard systems in Armenia" (HosDog only with permission). "That's why we offer a paid pilot instead of asking you to trust us." |

## 8. Close and next step
- **Ask:** «Փորձենք 30 օր Ձեր իրական տվյալներով։ Պիլոտը 100 000 ֏ է, ամբողջությամբ նախապես, ամսավճար չկա պիլոտի ընթացքում։ Կարո՞ղ ենք սկսել [date]-ին» / «Давайте попробуем 30 дней на ваших реальных данных. Пилот — 100 000 ֏ предоплатой, без абонплаты на время пилота. Начнём [дата]?»
- **If yes:** send the pilot agreement + invoice the same day. Agree the success criteria. Book a 60-minute onboarding.
- **If "need to think":** agree a date for a 10-minute follow-up call. Leave the one-page leave-behind with the QR to the demo.
- **CRM:** log the meeting in `docs/RISEUP_SALES_SYSTEM/RISEUP_SALES_CRM.xlsx` (stage, next action, follow-up date, expected payment). The deal is won only after the payment is verified.
