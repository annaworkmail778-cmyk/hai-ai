# 03 — Business Strategy

## Recommendation

**Sell one focused offer to one segment first: the Developer Sales OS to Armenian residential developers.** Run a lighter Solar Operations OS track in parallel, aimed at installers and O&M companies. In both, sell a **paid pilot with a deposit**, not a large platform. Keep the SMB productized services, including the contest funnel, as a **cash bridge** during the 40 days.

| | |
|---|---|
| Alternatives considered | (a) Stay SMB-only. (b) Go straight to EU/US. (c) Build a SaaS first, then sell |
| Why this | Developer deals are 10–20× SMB ticket sizes, so a few deals move the $6k target. The construction market is growing (doc 04). The founder already has a real estate automation project. EU/US needs case studies first. SaaS-first delays cash |
| Cost | Close to zero in tools. The cost is founder time |
| Effort | High in sales effort, moderate in build effort (hybrid stack) |
| Risks | B2B sales cycles may run longer than 40 days. RiseUp has no developer references yet. The founder's planned move to Vietnam limits in-person meetings |

## Offers

| Offer | Problem it solves | Pilot scope (first 30 days) | Proposed price (assumption, approve in doc 21) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Developer Sales OS** | Leads from Instagram, Facebook, website, phone and WhatsApp get lost or answered slowly. Managers can't see the pipeline, and follow-ups are inconsistent | Unified inquiry capture (2–3 channels) → AI qualification questions → lead list/CRM → instant rep notification → follow-up reminders → weekly management dashboard | Setup **$2,000–3,500** (≈ 780k–1.36M AMD), monthly **$150–300**. 50% deposit |

Alerts, service visits and client reporting are inconsistent | Pull inverter data where an official API or export exists → alerts to Telegram → service ticket list → monthly client report. Pilot on ≤10 sites | Setup **$1,000–2,000**, monthly **$100–250**. 60% upfront |
| **Enterprise Automation** | Repetitive manual processes such as reports, approvals and data entry | One workflow, fixed scope | From **$800–2,000** per workflow, 50/50 |
| **Brokerage / referral** (secondary) | Connecting a client with a vetted vendor (e.g. a solar installer) | Written referral agreement, disclosed to both sides | 5–10% of the first contract, paid by the vendor after the client pays. Doc 16 covers the rules |
| **SMB services** (existing) | AI admin, websites, AI video | As on the site | Existing AMD price list |

**Why pilots.** No case studies exist yet. A paid, fixed-scope pilot with a clear success metric lowers the buyer's risk and gets RiseUp its first reference. Example metrics: median first-response time, or the share of leads with a logged follow-up.

**Pricing benchmark.** Russian-market integrators quoted roughly 250–500k RUB for a basic Bitrix24 CRM implementation, and about 1M RUB or more with 1C integration (doc 05). That source is undated. Armenian prices are unknown and need checking before the first proposal (doc 21).

## Positioning

- **Say:** "We connect your channels, answer and qualify every inquiry in seconds, and give management one view of the pipeline. Pilot in 30 days."
- **Don't say:** "AI replaces your sales team", "guaranteed X% more sales", or "#1 in Armenia". There is no evidence for any of these.
- **Differentiation** from Bitrix24 and Profitbase integrators: messaging-first (Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp), Armenian-language AI replies, a fast pilot, and no seat licences required. Weakness: RiseUp has no chessboard or inventory module like Profitbase. Position as complementary to the client's CRM, not a replacement.

## Go-to-market (Armenia first)

1. **Sources of target companies** (legal sources only):
   - The public company register at e-register.moj.am.
   - Developers' own websites and project listings.
   - Real estate portals' developer pages, viewed manually.
   - Construction and energy news on arka.am, armenpress and pv-magazine.
   - armeps.am procurement notices.
   - Industry associations and events. The associations are unknown and need researching.
2. **Channels, in order:**
   1. Warm introductions from the founder's network and past clients.
   2. A phone call to the company's public sales number.
   3. A personalized email to a business address with an opt-out.
   4. A LinkedIn connection with a note.
   5. Instagram DM to the company account.
   
   No bulk messaging, no scraped lists and no purchased databases.
3. **Proof:**
   - The Developer Sales OS demo video.
   - A live sandbox demo.
   - A personalized 60-second video for A-tier prospects, using only the prospect's public name and no logo without permission.
4. **EU/US:** after 2–3 Armenian references. Doc 04 covers this.

## What we deliberately do not do

- Do not build a full custom CRM for clients. Integrate with Sheets, HubSpot, Zoho or Bitrix24 instead.
- Do not chase public tenders as the main channel in the 40-day window. Procurement cycles are slow, and RiseUp would need registration and documents first.
- Do not run commission deals that hide fees from either party.
