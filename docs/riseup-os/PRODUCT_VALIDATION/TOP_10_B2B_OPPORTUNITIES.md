## PART 1 — Executive conclusion

**Strongest overall theme:** *revenue recovery for businesses that already have money "stuck" in their own data.* There are three places it sits:

1. **Past customers who stopped coming back.** This is the primary opportunity: a done-for-you win-back campaign.
2. **Overdue B2B invoices.** This is the secondary opportunity: a receivables follow-up autopilot.
3. **Orders that staff retype by hand from WhatsApp, Telegram, email or phone.** This is the backup: AI order intake for distributors.

**Why these beat the previous "AI receptionist for clinics" idea:**
- They sell a number the owner already cares about: returning customers, cash collected, and order-desk hours saved. "Faster replies" is not that kind of number.
- Each one has international proof that businesses pay real money for it:
  - reactivation agencies charge about $75–150 per booked appointment [FACT S4];
  - Chaser's receivables tool starts at about $259/month [FACT S2], and Upflow's average contract is about $15,000/year [FACT S2];
  - AI order-entry tools cost $1,000+/month (OrderSync) [FACT S11], and WizCommerce raised $8M for AI wholesale ordering [FACT S11].
- Locally, there are few or no direct providers [HYPOTHESIS]. Our existing skills (n8n, LLMs, WhatsApp/Telegram, Sheets, dashboards) cover the MVPs in 3–7 days.
- Opportunities #2 and #3 sell to **the same buyer**: the owner or finance lead of a distributor or wholesaler. One prospect list carries two offers.

**Honest probability of $5,000 received in 30 days** [ESTIMATE]:
- Any single idea: about 5–20%.
- The recommended combined plan (win-back campaigns + one B2B deposit + existing warm leads): **about 25–30%**.

The constraint is not the product but conversion. The team's history is 300–400 cold calls with 0 sales (`docs/plan-60-days.md`). The plan therefore relies on **paid, short, outcome-measured offers** sold **face to face and through warm introductions**.

## PART 3 — Detailed analysis of the top 5

### #1 Customer Win-Back Campaigns

**A. Concept**

Done-for-you campaigns that bring back customers who bought or visited once and stopped.
- **Who:** businesses with **≥ 500 past customers on record** and a natural repeat cycle: dental and aesthetic clinics, auto service centres, salons, fitness studios, optics, veterinary clinics, language schools, B2C home services.
- **Where:** Armenia first; Georgia and Russian-speaking diaspora businesses second.

**B. Problem**
- Most small businesses never systematically contact lapsed customers. Lists sit in booking apps, Excel, POS systems or phones [HYPOTHESIS: validate in discovery].
- Today they do nothing, or post generic Instagram promos.
- Re-acquiring a past customer costs less than ads [widely held; ESTIMATE].
- The loss is the revenue of customers who would have returned if invited.

**C. Paying-customer evidence**
- US/UK agencies sell this as "database reactivation": per-appointment fees of $75–150 for local services, flat 30-day campaign fees, or setup + revenue share [FACT S4].
- Pricing analyses warn that pure per-meeting deals are often unprofitable; hybrids dominate [FACT S4].
- Local evidence: **none found** [HYPOTHESIS].

**D. Solution**

What we build:
1. **List import and cleaning.** Excel, a booking-system export or a POS export → dedupe → segment by last visit, service and value.
2. **Consent check.** Only contacts with a lawful basis or opt-in; others are excluded, or handled by the client's own staff calls.
3. **Personalized offers and messages** in hy/ru/en, approved by the owner.
4. **Sending through compliant channels:** WhatsApp Cloud API marketing templates (opt-in; per-message fee) [FACT S7], SMS via a local provider (cost **NEEDS VALIDATION**), email, and **AI-prioritized call lists + scripts for the client's own administrator**.
5. **Replies → booking** through the existing AI admin flow + Calendar.
6. **Results report:** contacted, replied, booked, came in, revenue (from the client).

**Do not build:** a SaaS platform, an automated mass-SMS engine, or Instagram outreach. Instagram cannot message users outside the 24-hour/7-day windows [FACT S13].

**Differentiation:** local language, done-for-you, and a results report tied to bookings. It is a campaign, not software to learn.

**E. Commercial model** [ESTIMATE]
| Item | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Win-back campaign | **150,000–250,000 AMD** | Paid upfront. Includes setup, 1 campaign (2 waves), report. Message fees at cost |
| Performance bonus (optional) | 3,000–5,000 AMD per returned customer who paid | Invoiced after the campaign. Lets us price the base lower |
| Monthly retention program | 60,000–90,000 AMD/month | Monthly segments: lapsed, birthdays, post-treatment check-ups |
| Add-on | Inquiry recovery (#5), 150,000–250,000 AMD | Upsell |

**Assumption:** priced so that 3–5 returning customers repay the campaign for a clinic or auto service [HYPOTHESIS: test against their average ticket].

**F. Economics** [ESTIMATE]
- **Delivery:** 1.5–3 person-days per campaign. Direct costs are messaging fees (client pays) and LLM ≈ $1–5.
- **Gross margin:** about 85–90%.
- **MRR:** $2,000 MRR ≈ 9–13 retention clients at 60–90k AMD. $10,000 MRR ≈ 45–65 clients, which is not reachable with two people without productizing.
- **Implementation:** 3–5 days from receiving the list.

**G. Sales**
- **Contact:** the owner or manager.
- **Find them:** Google Maps categories (manual), Instagram business pages, and the existing contest participant list.
- **Reach them:** in-person visit, warm intro, then a phone call.
- **Message:**
  > «Ձեր հին հաճախորդների բազայից մենք կվերադարձնենք մարդկանց, ովքեր վաղուց չեն եկել։ Վճարում եք մեկ արշավի համար, իսկ արդյունքը տեսնում եք ամրագրումներով 2 շաբաթում»
  
  ("We will bring back people from your old customer list who haven't come in a long time. You pay for one campaign and see the result in bookings within 2 weeks.")
- **Paid ads:** not needed.
- **Sales cycle:** days to 2 weeks.
- **First 3 customers:** existing warm contacts (HosDog; past contacts from the AI admin and contest), plus in-person visits to auto service centres and clinics with large lists.

**H. MVP (3–5 days)**
- **Build:** a list cleaner (Sheets + n8n), a message generator (LLM), a sending adapter (WhatsApp Cloud API or email; SMS provider TBD), a reply-to-booking flow (reuse) and a report template.
- **Demo:** a before/after sample report with **illustrative data**, plus a live message → reply → booking flow.
- **Blockers:** WhatsApp business verification and template approval lead time; local SMS provider terms; consent.

**I. Why us**
- **SaaS:** owners won't run campaigns themselves.
- **Local agencies:** they sell ads, not their own customer data.
- **An internal employee:** has no time or tooling.
- **A freelancer:** no results report or booking integration.
- **Doing nothing:** the money stays lost.

**J. Risks**
- **Legal:** Armenian advertising/consent rules and Meta opt-in. **Lawyer check before the first send.** If consent is lacking, switch to staff-call lists.
- Lists may be poor (no phone numbers or dates).
- Results variance. Mitigation: price the base low with a performance bonus.
- Reputation risk if messaging is spammy. Mitigation: low frequency and owner approval.

### #2 Receivables Autopilot (overdue B2B invoices)

**A. Concept**

Automated, polite, escalating follow-up of unpaid invoices, plus a weekly cash-in forecast for the owner.
- **Who:** Armenian B2B SMEs with 30+ active customers on credit terms: distributors and wholesalers, building-material suppliers, logistics firms, printing, B2B services, agencies.

**B. Problem**
- Late payments tie up cash. Chasing is manual, awkward and inconsistent, and is often done by the owner or accountant via phone and WhatsApp [HYPOTHESIS].
- Today: Excel aging lists from accounting software, plus ad-hoc calls.
- The cost is cash-flow strain, credit-line interest and staff time.

**C. Evidence**
- Chaser from ~$259/month flat [FACT S2].
- Upflow quote-based, with an average contract of ~$15,000 [FACT S2].
- Chaser's own report claims customers using AR automation are 52% more likely to be paid within 2 weeks. This is a vendor claim and is unverified [S2].
- Armenia: invoices run through the State Revenue Committee e-invoicing system [FACT S14]. That is a data source we could potentially use (export or API access **NEEDS VALIDATION**).

**D. Solution**
- **Build:**
  1. An **aging import** (Excel export from the accounting system; Armsoft or 1C formats **NEEDS VALIDATION**).
  2. A reminder sequence per customer (before due, due, +3, +7, +14 days) by email and WhatsApp/Telegram, in hy/ru, with the invoice PDF and bank details.
  3. **Escalation tasks** to the owner/accountant for calls.
  4. A **promise-to-pay log**.
  5. A **weekly cash forecast** to Telegram.
- **Do not build:** payment processing, a collection agency service, or direct accounting-system write-back.

**E. Commercial model** [ESTIMATE]
- Setup **300,000–500,000 AMD**: 50% upfront, 50% at go-live.
- Monthly **50,000–100,000 AMD**, priced by number of debtors.
- Add-on: a credit-limit and customer risk report.
- Custom: accounting-system integration, from 300,000 AMD.

**F. Economics** [ESTIMATE]
- **Delivery:** 4–7 days. Direct costs are minimal (email, WhatsApp fees, LLM < $5/month).
- **Gross margin:** about 85%.
- **MRR:** $2,000 MRR ≈ 8–15 clients. $10,000 MRR ≈ 40–75 clients.

**G. Sales**
- **Decision makers:** owner, CFO or chief accountant.
- **Find them:** public lists of distributors and wholesalers, building-material markets, business associations, and accountants' referrals. **Accounting firms are a partner channel** [HYPOTHESIS].
- **Message:**
  > «Քանի՞ միլիոն դրամ է հիմա ուշացած Ձեր հաճախորդների մոտ։ Մենք ավտոմատացնում ենք հիշեցումները, և Դուք ամեն երկուշաբթի տեսնում եք, թե ով երբ կվճարի»
  
  ("How many million drams are overdue with your customers right now? We automate the reminders, and every Monday you see who will pay when.")
- **Sales cycle:** 1–4 weeks.
- **First 3:** warm network and accountant referrals.

**H. MVP (4–6 days):** an Excel aging import → n8n sequences → email/Telegram/WhatsApp → a Sheets log + a weekly report.
- **Demo:** an illustrative aging list going through a reminder sequence to a forecast.
- **Blockers:** data access trust, accounting export formats, and the WhatsApp business number.

**I. Why us:** no local tool that we found [HYPOTHESIS]; Armenian/Russian tone and templates; fits the existing accounting setup; cheaper than Chaser/Upflow and done for them.

**J. Risks**
- Owners fear damaging relationships. Mitigation: owner-approved tone, B2B only, no harassment.
- Sensitive financial data. Mitigation: least access, a data processing agreement (DPA).
- Accounting formats vary.

### #3 AI Order Intake for distributors and wholesalers

**A. Concept:** customer orders arriving by WhatsApp, Telegram, email, photos of handwritten lists or voice notes are turned into structured, catalogue-matched orders. Staff confirm them in one click, and the system exports them to the accounting or ERP system.
- **Who:** Armenian food, beverage, household, building-material and pharma distributors with 50+ B2B customers ordering by messenger [HYPOTHESIS: validate how orders actually arrive].

**B. Problem:** order-desk staff retype orders and misread product names, so there are errors, delays and overtime. Today this is manual entry into 1C or Armsoft.

**C. Evidence**
- Orderlion's AI inbox reads email, WhatsApp, SMS and voicemail orders [FACT S11].
- OrderSync charges from $1,000/month + per-order fees [FACT S11].
- WizCommerce raised $8M for AI wholesale ordering [FACT S11].
- Manual cost per order: vendor estimates of €3–9 and $30–80 [S11; vendor claims, divergent].

**D. Solution**
- **Build:** intake channels (a dedicated WhatsApp Cloud API number or Telegram bot, plus an email inbox) → LLM extraction (customer, SKU, quantity, delivery date) → **fuzzy matching to the client's catalogue** → a review queue (Sheets or a simple web page) → confirmation message to the customer (approved) → **Excel/CSV export in the accounting system's import format**.
- **Do not build:** ERP replacement, pricing engines, a customer storefront.

**E. Commercial model** [ESTIMATE]
- **Paid discovery/pilot** (1 week, 1 channel, top 300 SKUs): 100,000–150,000 AMD upfront.
- **Setup:** 600,000–1,200,000 AMD, 50/50.
- **Monthly:** 80,000–150,000 AMD, by order volume.

**F. Economics** [ESTIMATE]
- **Delivery:** 1–2 weeks. LLM costs are small per order (< $0.01–0.05 per order, depending on the model; **measure in the pilot**).
- **Gross margin:** about 80%.
- **MRR:** $2,000 MRR ≈ 6–10 clients; $10,000 MRR ≈ 30–50 clients.

**G. Sales**
- **Decision makers:** owner and commercial director.
- **Find them:** wholesale markets, supplier lists of supermarkets and HoReCa, and referrals from #2 clients.
- **Message:**
  > «Ձեր հաճախորդները պատվերները գրում են WhatsApp-ով, իսկ աշխատակիցը ձեռքով մուտքագրում է։ Մենք դա անում ենք ավտոմատ, աշխատակիցը միայն հաստատում է»
  
  ("Your customers send orders by WhatsApp and an employee retypes them by hand. We do it automatically; the employee only confirms.")
- **Sales cycle:** 2–6 weeks.

**H. MVP (5–7 days):** a Telegram bot + email intake → extraction → catalogue match → Sheets review → CSV export.
- **Demo:** 20 illustrative messy orders → a clean order table, live.
- **Blockers:** catalogue quality, the accounting import format, and WhatsApp verification.

**I. Why us:** local language and handwritten Armenian/Russian product names [HYPOTHESIS: test accuracy]; we fit the existing ERP rather than replacing it; we deliver on site.

**J. Risks:** extraction accuracy (keep a human-in-the-loop always), integration effort, and a slower sales cycle.

### #4 Instant Quote Builder for custom manufacturers

**A. Concept:** turns inbound Instagram/WhatsApp requests ("a kitchen of 3.2 m, MDF, this colour") into a structured spec + a price range + a PDF quote within minutes, for furniture, kitchens, windows, doors and metalwork makers.

**B. Problem:** quotes take days and depend on one estimator. Slow quotes lose deals [HYPOTHESIS].

**C. Evidence:** quoting tools such as QuoteIQ ($29.99–699/month) and contractor tools from $49/user [FACT S9]. Local evidence: none.

**D/E/F.**
- **Build:** guided spec questions + the client's pricing rules (in a Sheet) → quote PDF + follow-up.
- **Price** [ESTIMATE]: 250–500k AMD setup + 40–70k AMD/month.
- **Delivery:** 1–2 weeks, because each pricing model is custom. **Margin:** about 75%.

**G.** Owners, reached through Instagram showrooms and furniture centres. Cycle 2–4 weeks.

**H.** MVP 5–7 days per client; the template is reusable only partly.

**I.** Speed and local language.

**J.** Pricing complexity, owner reluctance to publish prices, and a bespoke-build trap.

## Service vs SaaS vs Hybrid

| | Fastest 1st payment | Highest WTP | Lowest investment | Best margins | Best scalability | P($5k/30d) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Service first | **✔** | ✔ (outcome-priced) | **✔** | High (85–90%) at low volume | ✗ | **Highest** |
| B. SaaS first | ✗ (weeks to build, low prices) | ✗ (anchored to $29–500 tools) | ✗ | High eventually | **✔** | Lowest |
| C. Hybrid (paid setup + recurring) | ✔ (after the first) | ✔ | ✔ | High | Medium | High |

**Recommendation:** **A** for the first 30 days (campaign and setup fees), moving to **C** for every live client (a monthly retention or monitoring fee). **B only after 10+ paying clients with the same workflow.**

## PART 7 — Validation plan (top 3)

The prospect lists are **built by the team from public sources during validation**. No prospect names are invented here.

### V1 — Win-back campaigns
- **Profile:** a Yerevan B2C business with ≥ 500 past customers whose phone numbers are on record, a repeat-visit cycle, and an owner who is reachable.
- **List-building:** Google Maps (manual) for dental, aesthetic, auto service, salon, fitness, optics and veterinary businesses, plus the existing contest and warm lists. Build **40 prospects** with a source URL for each.
- **Problem to investigate:** how many customers haven't returned in 6+ months, whether contact data and consent exist, and the average ticket.
- **Outreach (hy):**
  > «Բարև Ձեզ։ Rise-ից եմ։ Օգնում ենք բիզնեսներին վերադարձնել այն հաճախորդներին, ովքեր վաղուց չեն եկել։ Ունե՞ք 10 րոպե, որ ցույց տամ, թե ինչպես է դա աշխատում և ինչքան կարող է արժենալ Ձեզ համար»
  
  ("Hello, I'm from Rise. We help businesses bring back customers who haven't come in a long time. Do you have 10 minutes for me to show how it works and what it could be worth to you?")
- **Discovery script:**
  1. How many customers are in your records?
  2. How many came back in the last 6 months?
  3. What is the average ticket?
  4. How do you contact customers today?
  5. Did customers agree to receive messages?
  6. What would 20 returning customers mean to you?
- **Demo:** an illustrative campaign report + a live message → reply → booking.
- **Paid pilot:** **one 2-wave campaign** on up to 1,000 contacts at **150,000 AMD** (also test 250,000 AMD), paid upfront, plus a 5,000 AMD bonus per returned paying customer.
- **Go:** ≥ 3 paid campaigns from the first 30 conversations within 14 days, **and** ≥ 1 campaign producing returns worth ≥ 3× its fee (client-confirmed).
- **No-go:** < 1 paid from 30 conversations; or > 60% of lists lacking usable consent or contacts.

### V2 — Receivables Autopilot
- **Profile:** an Armenian B2B firm with ≥ 30 credit customers and a recurring overdue problem.
- **List-building:** 30 distributors, wholesalers and B2B service firms from public directories and markets, plus **3 accounting firms as partners**.
- **Problem:** overdue amount and days, who chases, time spent.
- **Outreach (ru):**
  > «Здравствуйте! Компания Rise. Автоматизируем напоминания клиентам о просроченных счетах. Вы каждую неделю видите, кто и когда заплатит. Можно 15 минут показать?»
  
  ("Hello! Rise here. We automate reminders to your customers about overdue invoices, and every week you see who will pay and when. Could we show you in 15 minutes?")
- **Script:**
  1. How many customers are on credit?
  2. Overdue now (approximate)?
  3. Who chases, and how?
  4. Which accounting system and export do you use?
  5. What tone is acceptable?
- **Demo:** an illustrative aging list → a sequence → a forecast.
- **Paid pilot:** 30-day pilot on the top 30 debtors at **200,000 AMD** (credited to setup).
- **Go:** ≥ 2 paid pilots from 20 conversations in 21 days.
- **No-go:** < 1 paid, or owners refuse to share any receivables data.

### V3 — AI Order Intake
- **Profile:** a distributor receiving ≥ 30 orders/day by messenger or email.
- **List:** 30 distributors (overlapping with V2), plus HoReCa suppliers.
- **Problem:** orders per day, channels, time per order, error rate.
- **Outreach (hy):**
  > «Ձեր պատվերների մեծ մասը գալի՞ս է WhatsApp-ով կամ Telegram-ով։ Ցույց կտանք, թե ինչպես դրանք ավտոմատ դառնում են պատրաստի պատվեր Ձեր համակարգի համար, 10 րոպեում»
  
  ("Do most of your orders arrive via WhatsApp or Telegram? In 10 minutes we'll show how they automatically become ready orders for your system.")
- **Demo:** paste 20 of their real (anonymised) orders → a structured table live. Accuracy is shown honestly.
- **Paid pilot:** 1-week discovery + pilot on 1 channel at **120,000 AMD**.
- **Go:** ≥ 2 paid discoveries from 15 conversations in 21 days **and** ≥ 90% extraction accuracy on their sample after review.
- **No-go:** accuracy < 80%, or no paid discovery from 15 conversations.

## PART 8 — What NOT to build (attractive but commercially weak now)

1. **A multi-tenant "AI receptionist/CRM" SaaS.** Saturated, anchored to $29–500/month global tools [S12].
2. **Rise Command Center or an internal dashboard.** No customer pays for it.
3. **An Armenian AI voice receptionist.** Speech quality unverified, high build cost.
4. **A generic chatbot or website service as the main business.** Commoditized; low prices locally (our own price list already shows 45–150k AMD).
5. **AI video ads as the core business.** Easy to replicate; low willingness to pay.
6. **A review-generation SaaS for Armenia.** US willingness to pay [S5] does not prove local demand.
7. **Shopify apps / international e-commerce support.** Saturated (Gorgias ~$0.90–1.00/resolution [S8]), and we have no references.
8. **LinkedIn scraping or automation tools.** Terms-of-service and legal risk.
9. **Mass SMS or WhatsApp blasting.** Illegal without consent; risks platform bans.
10. **ERP or accounting replacement for distributors.** Integrate instead; never replace.
11. **Short-term-rental messaging tools.** Cheap SaaS exists (Hospitable $29–99/month [S17]).
