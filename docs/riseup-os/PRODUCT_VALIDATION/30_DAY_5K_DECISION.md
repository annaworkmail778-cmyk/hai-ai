## 1. Executive summary: the decision

**Pursue first:** become a **white-label "AI-speed" build and automation partner for small digital, marketing and automation agencies**. We build websites, web apps, client dashboards, n8n/GoHighLevel automations and AI assistants that *the agency* has already sold to *its* clients.

**Why this beats the alternatives for *this* 30-day goal:**

1. **The buyer already has the money and the urgency.** An agency that sold a project needs it delivered now. We don't have to convince an end business of ROI, which is where earlier local sales failed (300–400 cold calls, 0 sales).
2. **Spending is visible and recurring.**
   - Agencies routinely post paid GoHighLevel/automation build work on Upwork, at fixed $500–1,500 per build and ongoing retainers of 10–15 hours a month [MARKET S1].
   - White-label web development has an established price ladder: Eastern European rates of about $25–65/hour [MARKET S2]; a 5-page WordPress build costs the agency about $800–2,500 and is resold at about $2,500–7,500 [MARKET S2].
3. **Payment risk is solvable.** Escrow-funded milestones on Upwork, or 50% upfront on direct contracts. That matters because Stripe is unavailable to Armenian merchants and PayPal can't receive commercial payments [FACT S9].
4. **It fits our real strengths:**
   - fast AI-assisted building (Claude Code);
   - n8n/ManyChat/CRM integrations;
   - a strong portfolio we already own (riseup-group.com, the Lead-to-Booking, Developer Sales OS and Solar demos, the Remotion video pipeline);
   - Person 2 can run written English outreach.
5. **It can become recurring:** a monthly "capacity" retainer.

**Honest probability** [ASSUMPTION]:
- **~25%** of collecting $5,000 in 30 days with this alone.
- **~30–35%** if we add the secondary offer (Kommo/amoCRM implementations for Russian-speaking SMBs abroad) and keep the existing local warm leads alive.
- The realistic outcome is **$3,000–4,500 collected**.

Nothing we found gives a credible >50% chance from a standing start with no reviews.

**Secondary:** Kommo (amoCRM) + WhatsApp/Telegram/Instagram + AI-bot implementations for **Russian-speaking SMBs outside Russia** (Armenia, Georgia, Kazakhstan, UAE, Cyprus, Israel).
**Backup / high-ticket:** fixed-price **MVP and internal-tool builds, and "vibe-coded app rescue" sprints** for non-technical founders.

## 12. Risks and failure scenarios
1. **Trust / no reviews:** agencies fear unreliable subcontractors. Mitigation: a paid small test, demos, fast response, NDAs, white-label confidentiality.
2. **Price race to the bottom** (Upwork GHL postings start at $4–35/hour [S1]). Mitigation: sell quality and AI/automation depth, and target agencies that value design.
3. **English B2 on live calls.** Mitigation: async-first (Looms, written briefs), and Person 2 drafts all written English.
4. **Agencies paying net-30.** Mitigation: 50% upfront or escrow for new vendors; prepaid retainers.
5. **Scope creep.** Mitigation: a written brief and a change-request rate.
6. **Platform risk** (Upwork terms, account review). Mitigation: comply; never move clients off-platform to avoid fees.
7. **Payment rails:** no Stripe or PayPal for Armenian merchants [S9]. Mitigation: Upwork payouts, Payoneer, bank transfer invoices.
8. **Spreading too thin.** Mitigation: two tracks only.
