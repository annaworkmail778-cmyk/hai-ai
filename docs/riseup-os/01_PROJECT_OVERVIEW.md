# 01 — Project Overview

## In one paragraph

RiseUp wants to move from selling small productized services to Armenian SMBs to selling **B2B revenue systems**. The SMB services are the AI admin, websites and AI videos. The B2B systems capture, qualify and follow up on leads and report on them for companies with larger ticket sizes. Two segments come first: real estate developers and solar/energy companies. To sell this way, RiseUp also needs an **internal Sales OS**: a disciplined, partly AI-assisted way to find, research, qualify, contact and close those companies, run by two people. The near-term financial goal is **$6,000 in cash actually received within 40 days**. That goal is a target, not a forecast. Doc 18 shows the scenarios in which it is reached and the ones in which it is not.

## Two things, not one

| | Internal Sales OS | Client products |
|---|---|---|
| Who uses it | Founder + Research & Ops | Developers, solar companies, other businesses |
| Purpose | Find and win clients | What RiseUp sells |
| Products | 8 modules (doc 08) | **Developer Sales OS**, **Solar Operations OS**, Enterprise Automation |
| Built with | Google Sheets + Claude Code agents + existing n8n/Telegram, mostly manual at first | Hybrid: n8n + messaging channels + client's CRM or Sheets + a RiseUp dashboard |
| When to automate | Only steps that prove repetitive (doc 19) | Per signed client |

The internal Sales OS doubles as the **live demo** of what RiseUp sells. That is the main reason to build it carefully. It is not a separate product.

## Priority markets (from the brief)

1. **Real Estate & Construction**, with the Developer Sales OS. This is the primary market.
2. **Energy & Solar**, with the Solar Operations OS.
3. **Enterprise Automation**: custom workflows for mid-size companies.
4. **Brokerage**, only as a secondary line and only where legitimate. This means referral or partner fees under written agreements, disclosed to all parties (doc 16).

## What RiseUp can truthfully claim today

The repo and the founder's own reports support the following. Nothing beyond this list should be claimed.

- A working **AI admin**: n8n + ManyChat on Instagram and Telegram, booking into Google Calendar, data into Google Sheets, and team notification via Telegram.
- A production website, riseup-group.com, in 3 languages, with a working Telegram lead notification.
- Delivered web projects: **hosdog.am**, with ordering, a dashboard and receipt printing, plus a real estate site auto-updated from a WhatsApp group and the Babasyan portfolio. All of these are founder-reported. Permission to name them in sales material is an open question (doc 21).
- **Solar-related experience:** one automation for Shtigen, sending hourly inverter-limit messages to Telegram. This is founder-reported and is not in the repo. It is the only solar reference, and it must not be described as a "solar platform".
- A Remotion-based video pipeline, used for the Lead-to-Booking demo and the contest video.

What RiseUp does **not** have yet: developer or solar clients, case studies with numbers, a CRM product, a dashboard product, or contracts. Every demo described in this folder must be labelled as a demo, with illustrative data.
