# 02 — $4,000 Cash Target Plan

**Target:** $4,000 ≈ **1,552,000 AMD actually received in the bank** (1 USD ≈ 388 AMD, assumption).
**Horizon:** 30 days, with the first cash aimed for in **days 3–10**.
This is a plan, not a forecast. Every number is an assumption until money arrives.

## 1. Four numbers, never mixed

| Term | Meaning | Counts toward $4,000? |
|---|---|---|
| Contract value | Total agreed price (setup + committed months) | **No** |
| Deposit | The upfront part, paid before work starts | Yes, **once it is in the bank** |
| Subsequent payments | On delivery, go-live, or monthly | Yes, **once received** |
| Actual cash received | Bank statement / cash receipt | **This is the only score** |

## 2. Speed ranking: what can pay first

| Source | Typical first payment | Time to first cash (assumption) |
|---|---|---|
| Balance on an almost finished project (#1 Babasyan, if paid) | Remaining balance | 1–5 days |
| Existing client upsell (#2, #3, #4) | 50% deposit | 3–10 days |
| Contest participants, small jobs (≤100k AMD) | 100% upfront | 3–14 days |
| Warm-intro developer pilot | 50% deposit | 14–30+ days |
| Cold developer outreach | — | Usually > 30 days. Don't count on it |

## 5. Payment rules that protect cash
- Small jobs (≤ 100k AMD): **100% upfront**, or 50% upfront + 50% before go-live.
- Bigger jobs: **50% deposit before any work starts.** No deposit, no start date.
- Monthly support starts from go-live, invoiced on the 1st, payable within 5 days.
- Payment by bank transfer to the Ameriabank account, or card or cash with a receipt. **Invoicing and tax depend on the sole-proprietor regime; confirm with an accountant.**
