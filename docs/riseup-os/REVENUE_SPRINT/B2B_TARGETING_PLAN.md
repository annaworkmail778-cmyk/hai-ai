# B2B Targeting Plan (14 days)

**Rule:** no prospect names are invented here. The team member builds the list from public sources and records a source URL and access date for every company. No purchased lists, no scraping against a site's terms, no bulk messaging.

## 1. Target segments and why

| Priority | Segment | Pain we solve | Entry offer | Realistic in 14 days? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Residential developers (Yerevan + Kotayk first) with an active sales project | Inquiries from many channels, slow replies, no pipeline view | Paid inquiry audit (150k, credited) → pilot 600–900k, 50% upfront | Only via warm intro or a fast decision maker |
| 2 | Solar installers / O&M companies | Faults found late, manual client reporting | Alerts pilot ≤ 10 sites, 200–250k, 60% upfront | **Yes**: small teams decide fast |
| 3 | Real estate agencies (5+ agents) | Lost WhatsApp/Instagram leads, no follow-up | CRM pilot 250–350k, 50% upfront | **Yes** |
| 4 | Construction companies (contractors) | Manual reports, material requests, site updates | One workflow, from 150k, 50% upfront | Possible |

## 2. Where to find companies (legal, manual)
- **Warm first:** the real estate client (ask which developers they work with), Shtigen (partners and suppliers), and the founder's 20-name network.
- Developers' own websites and their project pages; real estate portals' "new buildings / developers" sections (viewed manually).
- The Armenian company register: e-register.moj.am (verify legal name and status).
- Construction and energy news: arka.am, armenpress.am, pv-magazine (new projects = active buyers).
- Google Maps search ("solar panels installation Yerevan", "կառուցապատող", "недвижимость Ереван"), viewed manually.
- Facebook/Instagram business pages: active ad campaigns are a buying signal.

## 3. List format (in the tracker's B2B tab)
`company · segment · city · website · public phone/email (from their site only) · decision maker role (name only if publicly listed) · signal (new project / ads / hiring sales) · source URL · accessed date · score A/B/C · next action`

**Scoring (quick):**

| Points | Criterion |
|---|---|
| +3 | Warm intro available |
| +2 | Active sales project or ads now |
| +2 | Inquiries via Instagram/WhatsApp visible |
| +1 | Decision maker reachable by name |
| +1 | Size fits (1–5 projects / 10+ sites / 5+ agents) |

**A ≥ 6, B 4–5, C ≤ 3.** Contact A first.

## 4. Daily quotas (days 1–10)
| Who | Daily |
|---|---|
| T | 10 new companies researched (sourced) and 10 personalized first messages prepared |
| F | Approve and send 10 messages (manually), make 10 calls to public sales or office numbers, hold 1–2 meetings |

That is ≈ 100 B2B companies touched in 10 days. Expected (assumption): 10–15 conversations → 4–6 meetings → 2–3 proposals → **1–2 deposits**.

## 5. Contact sequence (B2B)
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 0 | Call the public sales or office number and ask for the person responsible for sales or marketing (developers, agencies) or operations (solar). Or a personalized email/DM if no phone |
| +1 | WhatsApp/email: 1-minute demo video + one-pager (doc 04) |
| +3 | Call: «Նայեցի՞ք։ 20 րոպե հանդիպե՞նք» |
| +6 | Last message, polite close |

**Meeting → proposal the same day → deposit invoice with the proposal.**

## 6. Paid entry offers that shorten the B2B cycle
1. **Developer Sales Inquiry Audit, 150,000 ֏, 100% upfront, 3 days.**
   - We map every inquiry channel, measure response times from their own data (provided by them), and show where leads are lost.
   - Deliverable: a 2-page report + the pilot plan.
   - The fee is credited against the pilot if signed within 14 days.
   - It is quick to sell, quick to deliver, and leads naturally into the pilot.
2. **Solar Alerts Pilot (≤ 10 sites), 200,000 ֏, 60% upfront, 7–10 days.**
3. **Agency CRM Pilot, 250,000 ֏, 50% upfront, 10 days.**

## 7. What not to do in 14 days
Public tenders (armeps.am) and EU/US outreach: too slow for this window. Large custom platforms: never start without a deposit.
