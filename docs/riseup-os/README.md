## Conventions in these documents

- **Fact** means a statement backed by a source in doc 22. Every source has an access date.
- **Assumption** means our own estimate. These are labelled, and the founder should check them.
- **Unknown** means it has not been researched or could not be verified. These are listed rather than guessed.
- Money is in USD unless stated otherwise. The working rate is **1 USD ≈ 388 AMD**, an assumption to confirm before pricing goes out.
- Nothing here claims customers, revenue, partnerships or capabilities that RiseUp does not have today.
