## Where RiseUp is weaker today

- No references in either target segment.
- Two people, so limited delivery capacity. A cap of about 3 concurrent pilots is recommended.
- No inventory or ERP depth, and no 1C experience (assumption; confirm in doc 21).
- The founder's relocation plans could reduce in-person presence, which matters to Armenian developers (assumption).

## Where RiseUp can win

- **Speed:** a working pilot in 2–4 weeks on existing tools.
- **Language:** Armenian, Russian and English AI replies, plus hy voice and video.
- **Video demos:** a strong, already-proven Remotion pipeline. Few local agencies sell with polished product demos (assumption).
- **Price:** a pilot below integrator project prices, with a monthly fee for support.
