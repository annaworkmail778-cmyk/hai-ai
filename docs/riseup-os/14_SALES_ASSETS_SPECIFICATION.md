## Deck outline
1. Title + one-line promise (no unverifiable claims).
2. The problem: inquiries across channels, slow replies, invisible pipeline.
3. Why now: the market is growing, the mortgage refund is phasing out, and buyers are more price-sensitive (sourced, doc 04).
4. How it works: the 6-step flow diagram.
5. Demo screenshots (badged "Demo").
6. What you get in the 30-day pilot.
7. What we measure: median response time, % of leads qualified, % with follow-up logged.
8. Works with your tools: Sheets, HubSpot, Bitrix24, Telegram.
9. Security and data: client owns the accounts, access is logged.
10. Pricing: pilot setup + monthly, payment terms.
11. About RiseUp: real facts only (team of 2, delivered projects with permission).
12. Next step.

## Discovery questions (developer)
- How many inquiries per week, and from which channels?
- Who answers, and how fast? How do you know?
- Where are leads stored today? Which CRM?
- What happens after the first reply? Who follows up?
- What does management see weekly?
- What would make a 30-day pilot a success for you?
- Who else decides? What is the budget process?

## Discovery questions (solar)
- How many sites, and which inverter brands?
- How do you learn about faults today?
- How are service visits tracked?
- Do you send clients reports?
- Do you have installer or API access on the inverter platforms?
