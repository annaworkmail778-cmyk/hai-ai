# 18 — Financial Model: $6,000 cash in 40 days

> **Not a forecast or a guarantee.** All conversion rates, prices and timings below are **assumptions** for planning. RiseUp has no B2B close-rate history yet. Update every Monday with real numbers.

Rate assumption: 1 USD ≈ 388 AMD → **$6,000 ≈ 2.33M AMD**.

## 1. Definitions
| Term | Meaning | Counts toward target? |
|---|---|---|
| Contract value | Total signed (setup + committed months) | No |
| Invoiced | Invoices issued | No |
| Deposit | Upfront payment, typically 50–60% | Yes, **once received** |
| Cash received | Money in the bank account within days 1–40 | **Yes, the only metric** |

## 2. Price assumptions (approve in doc 21)

|---|---|---|---|

Honest read: **the $6,000 target is reachable only if the base scenario holds,** meaning 3 B2B deals and steady SMB sales, and the first B2B deposit lands by around day 20. The biggest single risk is B2B sales cycle length.

## 4. Pipeline math (base scenario)

To sign 3 B2B deals in about 30 days:

| Step | Assumed conversion | Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Signed | — | **3** |
| Proposals sent | 30% → signed | **10** |
| Meetings held | 60% → proposal | **17** |
| Conversations (replies/calls) | 60% → meeting | **28** |
| Decision makers contacted | 25% → conversation | **≈ 110** |
| Companies researched (A/B) | ~1 contact per company | **≈ 110–130** |

Over 40 days that means 3–4 companies researched per day (feasible: target is 15–20/day in doc 11), about 3 first touches a day, and about 4 meetings a week. **Meetings in weeks 1–3 are the leading indicator.** If there are fewer than 6 meetings by day 14, the base scenario is unlikely, so shift effort to SMB and automation offers with shorter cycles.

## 5. Weekly checkpoints
| Day | On-track signal | If behind |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 60+ companies researched, 20+ contacted, 3+ meetings booked | More calls, use warm intros, lower first-touch friction (offer a 15-minute demo) |
| 14 | 6+ meetings held, 3+ proposals | Push SMB offers to contest participants and existing contacts |
| 21 | 1st B2B deposit received | Offer a smaller paid "discovery + prototype" ($500–800) |
| 30 | 2+ B2B signed, ≥$3,500 cash | Focus on collections and go-live milestones |
| 40 | Measure actual vs plan | Re-plan days 41–90 |

## 6. Costs in the window (assumption)
Tools ≈ $5–30 (doc 06) plus SMB ads 20–30k AMD (≈ $50–80), plus phone and transport. **Net cash ≈ cash received − ~$100.**

## 7. Personal cash needs (founder-reported)
The founder said about 150k AMD/month is needed and 60k of that is existing obligations. That is about $390/month. The **conservative** scenario covers this. The target exists to build a runway beyond it.
