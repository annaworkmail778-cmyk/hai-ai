# Product Catalog and Pricing

**Two price lists:**
- **International (USD)** for agencies in the UK, EU, US and Australia, and for Upwork.
- **Local (AMD)** for Armenia. Russian-speaking markets are priced in USD at about 70–85% of international [ASSUMPTION].

Prices are [ASSUMPTION], anchored to the evidence in `docs/riseup-os/PRODUCT_VALIDATION/30_DAY_5K_DECISION.md`:
- white-label rates of about $25–65/h;
- WordPress partner cost of about $800–2,500;
- GoHighLevel builds at $500–1,500 per Upwork postings;
- AI-assisted MVPs at $5–15k.

**Validate prices** in the first 10 conversations and adjust at Gate D10.

**General terms:**
- Written scope; 2 revision rounds.
- Change requests at $35/h (intl) or 12,000 AMD/h (local).
- Client owns the accounts.
- Third-party fees are paid by the client.
- Work starts after payment.
