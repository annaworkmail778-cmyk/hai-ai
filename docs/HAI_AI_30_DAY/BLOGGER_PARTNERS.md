## Qualification criteria (verify manually in the app)
| Criterion | Threshold [ASSUMPTION] | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Niche with purchase intent | Education/skills, fitness programs, nutrition, career/finance, parenting, beauty how-to, language learning | Audiences already pay for *outcomes* |
| Followers | 10k–150k | Big enough to sell, small enough to respond |
| Engagement | Comments show questions ("how do I…?") rather than only emojis | Demand signal |
| Already sells something | Consultations, paid chats, an existing guide | Proves the audience pays |
| Language | Armenian or Russian first | Our edge |
| Audience location | Can buy online (cards, local transfer) | Payment rails |

## Process (validation only; no building)
1. Shortlist 30 creators manually (Instagram/Telegram/YouTube search by niche + "Yerevan" / "Ереван"), with the profile URL as source.
2. Message 5 per week (scripts in `SALES_SCRIPTS.md`).
3. Offer the **paid validation sprint** ($300 / 120,000 AMD): a landing page + pre-sale.
4. Build only if: **≥ $500 pre-sold or ≥ 30 paid pre-orders**, **or** the creator pays a build fee.

**Stop at Day 14** if no creator agrees to a paid sprint, a pre-sale or a minimum guarantee.
