# RISEUP Business OS: Customer Discovery Kit (Tsaghkadzor)

**Purpose:** validate real problems and willingness to pay **before** building expensive integrations.

**Contents:**
- Questionnaires: §1 hotel, §2 restaurant
- 3-minute demo scripts: §3
- Feedback recording: §4–6

**Rules for every meeting:**
1. **Listen 70%, talk 30%.**
2. Ask about **past behaviour and real numbers** ("last weekend…"), not opinions about the future ("would you use…").
3. **Don't pitch features before you understand the problem.**
4. Write the owner's exact words.
5. Never promise results, features or dates you can't deliver.
6. Log everything in the CRM the same day. Hotels go in as Segment "Direct – Other", Business category "Hotel" / "Guesthouse"; restaurants as "Direct – Restaurant / Shop".

## 4. Feedback recording checklist (fill within 1 hour after every meeting)
- [ ] Business, person, role, **decision maker?** (yes/no/who)
- [ ] Size (rooms / tables), segment, current tools (booking tool, POS, Excel, paper)
- [ ] **Their volumes:** inquiries/week or orders/day; average booking value or average check
- [ ] **Top 3 pains, in their own words** (quotes), each rated for severity 1–5 by *them* ("how much does this bother you, 1–5?")
- [ ] **Evidence of cost:** a specific recent example (lost guest, wrong order, double booking)
- [ ] What they do today to solve it, and what that costs (time, money, people)
- [ ] Demo reaction: which moment got the strongest reaction? What confused them?
- [ ] Features they asked for (record; **don't promise**), and why they want them
- [ ] Objections (exact words)
- [ ] Price reaction: did they ask the price? Reaction to the pilot fee?
- [ ] **Evidence level reached** (§5)
- [ ] Next step + date; CRM updated (stage, next action, follow-up date, expected payment)

## 5. Evidence ladder (only levels 4–5 count as validation)
| Level | Signal |
|---|---|
| 0 | Polite interest, compliments ("nice, interesting") |
| 1 | Shared a real pain with a concrete example |
| 2 | Shared numbers (volumes, losses, costs) |
| 3 | Asked for price or timing, or booked a second meeting with the decision maker or data |
| 4 | Accepted the pilot proposal in writing |
| 5 | **Paid the pilot** (verified in the bank) |

## 7. Decision rules (recommended; review every Sunday)
| Rule | Threshold | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Build restaurant Stage 2 (≈ 11 h) | ≥ 3 restaurant interviews with an order/kitchen pain at severity ≥ 4 | Build; otherwise keep the hours for paid work |
| Continue the hotel offer as is | After 10 hotel meetings: ≥ 2 at level 5, or ≥ 5 at level ≥ 3 | Continue; prepare a Level 2 pilot build for paying clients only |
| Rework the offer | After 10 meetings in a vertical: < 2 at level ≥ 3 | Change the segment (e.g. guesthouses vs. hotels), the message or the pilot scope before building more |
| Add a feature to the roadmap | Requested independently by ≥ 3 businesses **and** tied to a pain at severity ≥ 4 | Estimate; build only for a paying pilot |
| Don't build | Requested by 1 business, or "nice to have" | Record only |
