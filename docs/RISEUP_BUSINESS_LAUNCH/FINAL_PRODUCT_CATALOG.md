## A. White-label partnerships (primary channel)
**Positioning:** "RISEUP is your external technology implementation team. We help agencies deliver more client projects without hiring additional developers. We build websites, web applications, CRM integrations, business automation and AI solutions under your brand."

**Targets:** CRM partners (Bitrix24/Kommo/GoHighLevel), digital marketing agencies, web studios (overflow), automation/AI agencies, IT consultancies, small software studios.

| Code | Offer | Deliverables | Excluded | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **WL-0** | **Paid Test Task** | One item: a landing page (1 page), one dashboard screen, or one workflow (≤ 8 nodes, 1 integration), under the partner's brand | Copywriting, brand design, more than 1 integration | 3–5 working days |
| WL-1 | Landing Page | 1 page, responsive, form → CRM/email, basic SEO, deploy | Copy, photos, more than 1 language | 4–6 days |
| WL-2 | Website (up to 6 pages) | Next.js or the partner's CMS (WordPress/Webflow on request), forms → CRM, SEO basics, speed, 1 language (+fee per extra language) | Copy, e-commerce, hosting fees | 7–10 days |
| WL-3 | Automation and AI Implementation | Up to 3 connected workflows (n8n/GoHighLevel/Make) **or** 1 AI assistant (web/Instagram/Telegram; WhatsApp via the official API) with knowledge base, human handoff and CRM write; error alerts; documentation; 7-day hypercare | Message/API/AI fees, WhatsApp verification paperwork | 5–10 days |
| WL-4 | Dashboard / Web App | Next.js + Supabase: auth, up to 5 screens/entities, 1 data integration, admin view, deploy, handover doc | Native mobile, payment processing, more than 2 roles | 2–3 weeks |
| **WL-R** | **Monthly Development Capacity** | Reserved hours, priority queue, 48-hour first response, shared board, monthly report; rollover ≤ 25% once | Unlimited work; work beyond the hours (billed hourly) | Monthly |

**How it works:**
1. Paid test.
2. Fixed quote per brief.
3. Projects.
4. Capacity retainer.

The partner owns the client relationship. RISEUP never contacts the end client unless the partner asks (see the White-Label Partnership Agreement).

## C. Upwork / Contra
**Profile headline:** "White-label web & automation builds — Next.js dashboards · n8n/Make · GoHighLevel · AI assistants".

**Categories (in order):**
1. n8n/Make automations and API/CRM integrations.
2. GoHighLevel builds.
3. Next.js/React landing pages and dashboards.
4. AI chatbot/assistant builds.
5. Fixing and finishing AI-built apps (Lovable/Bolt/Supabase).

**Job filters:**
- payment verified;
- hire history;
- posted < 24 h;
- fixed price ≥ $300 or hourly ≥ $25;
- clear scope;
- < 20 proposals.

**Always** use milestones/escrow; stay on the platform for payment with clients met there.
