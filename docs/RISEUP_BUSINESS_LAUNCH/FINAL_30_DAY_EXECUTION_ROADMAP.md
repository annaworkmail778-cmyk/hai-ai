## 2. Time allocation (starting hypothesis; re-balanced every Friday)
| Channel | Share of selling time | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| A. White-label agencies (Armenia 30% / RU-speaking 25% / Intl 45% of messages) | **50%** | B outreach, A calls |

**Reallocation rule:** move 10–20 points to the channel that produced **cash or paid tests** that week. Stop creators at Day 14 without a paid sprint or pre-sale.

## 4. Decision gates and pivot rules

| Gate | Success | Failure | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D3** | ≥ 60 messages sent, ≥ 3 replies, warm leads all contacted, ≥ 2 calls booked | < 2 replies | Rewrite the first message (shorter, a more specific demo); check deliverability (warm up the sending email; use LinkedIn) |
| **D5** | ≥ 1 payment received (any channel) **or** ≥ 2 paid-test invoices sent | 0 invoices sent | Lower the paid test to $250 / 90k AMD. Person A makes 10 direct calls to Armenian marketing agencies |
| **D7** | ≥ $300 received, ≥ 8 positive replies, ≥ 5 calls | < $150 and < 4 calls | Shift 20% of time from the weakest market to the best-replying market. Upwork: change the profile headline to the single niche with the most views |
| **D10** | ≥ $1,000 received, ≥ 1 project deposit | < $500 | Drop the worst agency market entirely. Direct channel: offer warm leads a smaller fast package. Consider the GoHighLevel-only niche on Upwork |
| **D14** | ≥ $2,000 received, ≥ 2 paying clients, ≥ 1 repeat or retainer conversation | < $800 | **Pivot primary to whichever channel produced money** (e.g., if Upwork paid and agencies didn't, make Upwork 50%) |
| **D21** | ≥ $3,500 received, pipeline of proposals ≥ $3,000 | < $2,000 | Stop new outreach in weak segments; 100% on closing + collection + upsells |
| **D30** | ≥ $5,000 received | — | Report cash vs signed vs outstanding vs pipeline vs MRR; plan the next 30 days |

**Stop rules:**
- **Bloggers:** stop at D14 if no creator agrees to a paid pre-sale or minimum guarantee.
- **Any market:** stop at D10 with < 2 positive replies per 50 messages.
