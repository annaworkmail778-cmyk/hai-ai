## Pricing decisions
1. **Keep the published Armenian website prices** as **entry packages**. They are public, and contest participants were promised 20% off until the end of November. Do not raise them this month. **Add scope limits, fair-use limits and a minimum custom-project price**, and sell larger "Business" scopes above them.
2. **Separate the price lists:** Armenia direct (AMD), Armenian agencies white-label (AMD), Russian-speaking international (USD, ~80% of EU/US), EU/US agencies (USD/EUR).
3. **Minimum custom project:** 150,000 AMD / $400. **Minimum paid test:** $250 / 100,000 AMD.
4. **Charged separately (at cost):** WhatsApp/Meta message fees, ManyChat/CRM/Bitrix/Kommo licences, domain, paid APIs beyond fair use, ad budgets.
5. **Hosting + AI usage** are included in direct monthly fees up to fair use: ≤ 1,000 AI conversations/month per assistant and standard hosting. Above that, billed at cost + 10% [ASSUMPTION: review after the first 3 clients].
6. **No unlimited revisions:** 2 rounds included; change requests are billed (see rates).

## Delivery-cost basis
- Internal labour shadow cost **$12/h** (≈ 4,700 AMD/h) [ASSUMPTION].
- Tools ≈ $0–10 per project.
- Gross margin = (price − hours × $12 − direct costs) ÷ price.
